# Contributors

Halcyon is the work of many people over many years. This file credits them, and keeps them apart from the people who maintain this copy, so no one mistakes one for the other.

## Original contributors

These people wrote Halcyon. **None of them is involved in this copy,** and listing them here does not mean they endorse it.

### OpenSimulator

Halcyon began as a fork of OpenSimulator 0.6.5. Much of its code is the work of the OpenSimulator developers ("Contributors, http://opensimulator.org/" in the file headers), who are listed in OpenSimulator's own contributor file: <http://opensimulator.org/>.
